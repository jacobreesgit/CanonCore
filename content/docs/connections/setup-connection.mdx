## What are connections?

Connections link CanonCore to your SFTP servers. Once connected, you can browse items, sync media files, and stream content directly from your server.

## Create a new connection

1. Click **Connections** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Connection**
3. Fill in your server details:
   - **Connection Name** - A friendly name like "My Server" or "Media Library"
   - **Host** - Your server address (e.g., `sftp.example.com`)
   - **Port** - Usually `22` (the default)
   - **Username** - Your SFTP username
   - **Authentication** - Choose password or SSH key
   - **Base Path** - The path to start from (e.g., `/media`)

4. Click **Create Connection**

## Test your connection

Before syncing items, test that everything works:

1. Find your connection in the list
2. Click the **Test** button
3. A green checkmark and latency time means you're connected

If the test fails, double-check your hostname, username, and password.

## Authentication options

**Password** - Enter your SFTP password. CanonCore encrypts this before storing it.

**SSH Private Key** - Paste your private key (the contents of your `id_rsa` or similar file). The key must be unencrypted - passphrase-protected keys aren't supported.

## Streaming configuration (optional)

For better media streaming performance, you can add WebDAV credentials. This lets CanonCore stream video directly instead of downloading first.

1. Click **Streaming Configuration** to expand the section
2. Enter your WebDAV details:
   - **WebDAV URL** - The HTTP endpoint for your server (e.g., `https://webdav.example.com/`)
   - **WebDAV Username** - Often the same as your SFTP username
   - **WebDAV Password** - Your WebDAV password

Not all servers support WebDAV. If yours doesn't, media still plays - it just downloads before playback.

## Edit or delete a connection

Click the three-dot menu on any connection card to:

- **Edit** - Update server details or credentials
- **Delete** - Remove the connection (this doesn't delete files on your server)
