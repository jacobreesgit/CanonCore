## How syncing works

When you sync, CanonCore scans your SFTP server and imports the structure as items. New items appear, and media files are catalogued for playback.

## Start a sync

### Sync a single connection

1. Go to **Connections** in the sidebar
2. Find the connection you want to sync
3. Click the **Sync** button on that connection's card
4. Wait for the sync to complete

The button shows your progress: **Sync** → **Syncing...** → **Synced**

After syncing, you'll see a summary like "3 added, 1 updated" showing what changed.

### Sync all connections at once

Want to update everything in one go?

1. Go to **Connections** in the sidebar
2. Click **Sync All** at the top of the page
3. All your connections sync in sequence

You'll see a summary showing how many connections synced and any errors that occurred.

## What gets synced

- **Items** on your server appear as navigable entries
- **Media files** (video, audio) are catalogued for streaming
- **Artwork** (images) are detected as thumbnails
- **Subtitles** (SRT, VTT) are linked to their media files

## View synced content

After syncing, you can browse items from a specific connection:

1. Go to **My Items** in the sidebar
2. Use the connection filter dropdown at the top
3. Select a connection to see only items from that server

Each item card shows a connection badge indicating which server it syncs from.

## Re-syncing

Click **Sync** again anytime to check for changes on your server. CanonCore compares timestamps and updates only what's changed.

## Troubleshooting

**Sync seems stuck?** Large libraries with many files take longer. Check your internet connection and try again.

**Files missing after sync?** Make sure the files are in a supported format. CanonCore recognizes common video (MP4, MKV), audio (MP3, FLAC), and image (JPG, PNG) formats.

**Changes not showing?** Run another sync to pick up new changes from your server. CanonCore compares timestamps to detect updates.
