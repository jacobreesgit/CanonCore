## Streaming media

CanonCore lets you play video and audio files directly from your SFTP server. After syncing, media files are ready to stream without downloading first.

## Supported formats

**Video:** MP4, MKV, AVI, M4V, WebM, MOV

**Audio:** MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, OGG

**Subtitles:** SRT, VTT, SUB, ASS

## Play a video

1. Open your connection and navigate to a folder with media files
2. Click on the folder to open its detail view
3. Find the video in the **Media Files** section
4. Click **Play** to open the full-screen player

If you've watched part of the video before, you'll see **Resume** instead of Play.

## Player controls

The video player includes:

- Play/pause
- Volume adjustment
- Progress bar for scrubbing
- Full-screen toggle
- Subtitle track selection

Click outside the player or press Escape to close it.

## Resume playback

CanonCore remembers where you stopped watching. When you return to a video, it picks up from your last position.

The detail view shows your progress - you'll see "45% watched" or similar next to each video.

## Subtitles

If your folder contains subtitle files, they appear automatically in the player. Click the subtitle icon in the player controls to choose a track.

For automatic detection, keep subtitle files in the same folder as the video.

## Primary files

When an item has multiple media files, you can choose which one plays by default through the [Item Settings](/docs/files-and-folders/item-settings) dialog.

1. Right-click the item and select **Settings**
2. Find the **Primary Media** section
3. Click on the file you want as the primary

The primary file plays when you click **Play** from the item detail view.

## Watch progress

The item detail view shows your progress on each file:

- A progress bar behind the file name
- Percentage watched label
- Completed videos (100%) show in green

This helps you track what you've seen across your media library.

## Troubleshooting

**Video won't play?** Some codecs inside container formats (like certain MKV files) may not work in all browsers. Try a different browser or re-encode the video.

**Subtitles not showing?** Make sure the subtitle file has a supported extension (.srt, .vtt, .sub, .ass) and is in the same folder.

**Playback buffering?** If you haven't configured WebDAV streaming, videos download before playing. Add WebDAV credentials in your connection settings for smoother streaming.
