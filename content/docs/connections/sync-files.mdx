## How syncing works

When you sync, CanonCore scans your SFTP server and imports the folder structure and file metadata. New folders appear, and media files are catalogued for playback.

## Start a sync

1. Open your connection
2. Click the **Sync** button in the toolbar
3. Wait for the sync to complete

The button shows your progress: **Sync** → **Syncing...** → **Synced**

After syncing, you'll see a summary like "3 added, 1 updated" showing what changed.

## What gets synced

- **Folders** on your server appear as navigable items
- **Media files** (video, audio) are catalogued for streaming
- **Artwork** (images) are detected as thumbnails
- **Subtitles** (SRT, VTT) are linked to their media files

## Synced content stats

After syncing, your connection page shows a stats panel with counts for:

- Folders
- Media files
- Artwork
- Subtitles

This gives you a quick overview of your library size.

## Re-syncing

Click **Sync** again anytime to check for changes on your server. CanonCore compares timestamps and updates only what's changed.

## Troubleshooting

**Sync seems stuck?** Large folders with many files take longer. Check your internet connection and try again.

**Files missing after sync?** Make sure the files are in a supported format. CanonCore recognizes common video (MP4, MKV), audio (MP3, FLAC), and image (JPG, PNG) formats.

**Changes not showing?** Navigate back to your connection root and sync again. Some nested changes may need a fresh sync.
