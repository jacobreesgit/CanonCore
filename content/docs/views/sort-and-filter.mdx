---
title: Sort and Filter
description: Organise how items appear in your list
---

Sort your items by name, date, or custom order. Filter to show only items that match specific criteria.

## Sort your items

Click the sort dropdown in the toolbar (it shows the current sort option, e.g. "Custom Order") to change how items are ordered. On mobile, tap **Options** in the toolbar to open a bottom sheet with sort, filter, and view options.

- **Custom Order** — Manual arrangement (drag and drop enabled)
- **Name A-Z** — Alphabetical order
- **Name Z-A** — Reverse alphabetical
- **Newest First** — Recently created items at top
- **Oldest First** — Oldest items at top
- **Recently Updated** — Most recently modified items first

### About custom order

Custom Order is the default. It lets you arrange items exactly where you want them using drag and drop. When you switch to any other sort option, drag and drop is disabled since the order is determined by the sort.

## Filter your items

Click the **Filter** button in the toolbar to open a multi-select dropdown with two groups of checkboxes.

### File Status

- **Has Files** — Only items with attached media, artwork, or subtitles
- **No Files** — Only items without any files

### Sync Status

- **Synced** — Items that are synced with Google Drive
- **Pending Sync** — Items waiting to sync
- **Sync Error** — Items that failed to sync

You can select multiple filters at once. Filters within the same group use OR logic (e.g. selecting both "Synced" and "Pending" shows items matching either status). Filters across groups use AND logic (e.g. "Has Files" + "Synced" shows only items that have files **and** are synced).

### Active filter indicator

When filters are active, the button shows the count — for example, **Filter (2)** means two filters are applied. A small dot also appears on the button.

### Clear filters

To remove all active filters, click the **Clear filters** button at the bottom of the dropdown. On mobile, tap **Clear all** in the filter section of the Options sheet.

If your filters don't match any items, you'll see a message with a button to clear them.

## Sort and filter together

Sorting and filtering work together. For example, you could filter to "Has Files" and sort by "Recently Updated" to see your most recently changed items that have files attached.

## Your choices are saved in the URL

CanonCore saves your sort, filter, and view mode in the page URL. This means you can bookmark or share a specific view — for example, a link with `?sort=name-asc&filter=has-files&view=grid` opens your library sorted alphabetically, filtered to items with files, in grid view.

When you navigate directly to a page without URL parameters, your last-used settings are restored from your browser's local storage.
