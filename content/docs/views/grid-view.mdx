Grid view displays your items as cards in a grid layout, making it easy to scan and find what you need.

## What grid view looks like

Your items appear as visual cards with a movie poster-style design. Each card shows:

- **Poster artwork** - When an item has TMDB metadata, the poster image loads directly from TMDB's servers. Items with custom artwork uploaded via Google Drive use that instead. Without either, the card shows the item's initial letter on a gradient background.
- **Item name** - Always visible at the bottom of the card
- **Hover details** - Hover over a card (or tap on mobile) to see the description and progress
- **Sync badge** - Shows sync status with Google Drive (if connected)
- **Media badges** — If the item has a video file, the card shows the duration (e.g., "1h 30m") and resolution (e.g., "1080p", "4K") as compact badges
- **Progress bar** - Shows how much you've watched (see [progress tracking](/docs/google-drive/progress-tracking))

Cards use a portrait aspect ratio and wrap automatically based on your screen size.

## Switch to grid view

1. Click the **View** dropdown in the toolbar (it shows the current view's icon)
2. Select **Grid** from the menu

On mobile, tap the options button in the toolbar and choose **Grid** from the **View** section.

## Working in grid view

### Open an item

Click on any item card to navigate inside it.

### Rearrange items

To reorder your items:

1. Click the **Edit** button to enter edit mode
2. Drag cards to rearrange them
3. Click **Done** when finished

Check out [drag and drop](/docs/views/drag-and-drop) for detailed instructions.

### Right-click for options

Right-click any card to see options like rename and delete.

## When to use grid view

Grid view works best when:

- You prefer a visual, spacious layout
- You're browsing and scanning for an item
- You have fewer items to display
- You find cards easier to read than lists

## Prefer a compact list?

Try [tree view](/docs/views/tree-view) for a hierarchical list layout that shows nested items all at once.

## Your preference is remembered

CanonCore remembers which view you prefer and uses it each time you visit.
