title: Drag and Drop
description: Rearrange your items by dragging them
---

Drag and drop is the fastest way to organize your items in CanonCore.

## Enter edit mode first

Before you can drag items, you need to enter edit mode:

1. Click the **Edit Mode** button in the top-right of your item list
2. You'll see drag handles appear on each item
3. When you're done, click **View Mode** to return to normal browsing

Edit mode shows simplified item icons to keep things focused on organization.

## How drag and drop works

1. **Click and hold** the drag handle on any item
2. **Drag** it to where you want it
3. **Release** to drop it in place

Your changes are saved automatically.

## What you can do

### Reorder items

Drag an item up or down to change its position in the list. Other items move out of the way as you drag.

### Nest items

Drag an item onto another item to move it inside. The target item will highlight when you hover over it.

### Move between levels

In tree view, drag items to change their position in the hierarchy.

## Tips for smooth dragging

- **Use the drag handle** - In tree view, look for the grip icon on the left. In grid view, look for it in the top-right corner of each card.
- **Watch the visual feedback** - Items will shift to show where your item will land
- **Drop on the highlight** - When nesting, make sure the target item is highlighted

## Works in both views

Drag and drop works in:

- **Tree view** - Reorder and nest within the visible hierarchy
- **Grid view** - Reorder cards at the same level

## Can't drag?

If you don't see drag handles:

- Make sure you've clicked **Edit Mode** to enter edit mode
- The button appears when you have items to organize

## Exit edit mode

When you're done organizing:

- Click the **View Mode** button
- Or switch between tree and grid view (this automatically exits edit mode)

## Undo a move

If you accidentally moved an item, just drag it back to where it was. All changes are immediate and there's no separate undo button.
