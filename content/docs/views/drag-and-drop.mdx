Drag and drop is the fastest way to organize your folders in CanonCore.

## How drag and drop works

1. **Click and hold** on any folder
2. **Drag** it to where you want it
3. **Release** to drop it in place

Your changes are saved automatically.

## What you can do

### Reorder folders

Drag a folder up or down to change its position in the list. Other folders move out of the way as you drag.

### Nest folders

Drag a folder onto another folder to move it inside. The target folder will highlight when you hover over it.

### Move between levels

In tree view, drag folders to change their position in the hierarchy.

## Tips for smooth dragging

- **Wait for the grab** - Click and hold for a moment before dragging
- **Watch the visual feedback** - Items will shift to show where your folder will land
- **Drop on the highlight** - When nesting, make sure the target folder is highlighted

## Works in both views

Drag and drop works in:

- **Tree view** - Reorder and nest within the visible hierarchy
- **Grid view** - Reorder cards at the same level

## Can't drop somewhere?

If a drop location doesn't seem to work:

- Make sure you're dragging to a valid location
- You can't drop a folder inside itself
- Try releasing in a slightly different position

## Undo a move

If you accidentally moved a folder, just drag it back to where it was. All changes are immediate and there's no separate undo button.
