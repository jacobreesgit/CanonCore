---
title: Tree View
description: See your folders in a hierarchical list
---

Tree view shows your folders as a nested, expandable list - just like a file explorer on your computer.

## What tree view looks like

```
📁 Work
  📁 Projects
    📁 Project A
    📁 Project B
  📁 Documents
📁 Personal
  📁 Photos
```

Folders with subfolders show an arrow you can click to expand or collapse.

## Switch to tree view

1. Look for the view toggle in the top-right of your folder list
2. Click the list icon to switch to tree view

## Working in tree view

### Expand and collapse folders

- Click the arrow next to a folder to show or hide its contents
- Click the folder name to navigate into it

### See the full hierarchy

- All your nesting levels are visible at once
- Easily understand your folder structure

### Drag and drop

- Drag folders to reorder them at the same level
- Drag onto another folder to nest it inside

## When to use tree view

Tree view works best when:

- You want to see your entire folder structure
- You're organizing and moving folders around
- You prefer a compact, list-style layout
- You have deeply nested folders
