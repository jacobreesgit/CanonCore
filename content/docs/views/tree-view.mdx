title: Tree View
description: See your items in a hierarchical list
---

Tree view shows your items as a nested, expandable list - just like a file explorer on your computer.

## What tree view looks like

Your items appear in a hierarchical list with indentation showing the nesting:

- Items with children show an arrow you can click to expand or collapse
- Item icons show artwork thumbnails when available
- Progress bars show how much you've watched in each item
- The full hierarchy is visible at once

## Switch to tree view

1. Click the **View** dropdown in the toolbar (it shows the current view's icon)
2. Select **Tree** from the menu

On mobile, tap the options button in the toolbar and choose **Tree** from the **View** section.

## Working in tree view

### Expand and collapse items

- Click the arrow next to an item to show or hide its contents
- Click the item name to navigate into it

### See the full hierarchy

- All your nesting levels are visible at once
- Easily understand your item structure

### Rearrange items

To reorder or nest your items:

1. Click the **Edit** button to enter edit mode
2. Drag items using the grip handle to reorder them
3. Drag onto another item to nest it inside
4. Click **Done** when finished

Check out [drag and drop](/docs/views/drag-and-drop) for detailed instructions.

### Right-click for options

Right-click any item to see options like rename, delete, and add child item.

## When to use tree view

Tree view works best when:

- You want to see your entire item structure
- You're organising and moving items around
- You prefer a compact, list-style layout
- You have deeply nested items

## Prefer visual cards?

Try [grid view](/docs/views/grid-view) for a more visual layout with item cards.
