Let's take a quick tour of CanonCore so you know where everything is.

## My Items

When you sign in, you land on My Items. This is where all your folders live.

### The Sidebar

On the left side, you'll find:

- **My Items** - Takes you back to your main folder list
- **Connections** - Manage your SFTP server connections
- **Get Help** - Opens this documentation
- **Quick Create** - Create a new folder from anywhere
- **Your profile** - Sign out and manage your account

### The Header

At the top of the page:

- **Breadcrumbs** - Shows where you are in your folder structure
- **Theme toggle** - Switch between light and dark mode

## Your First Folder

Ready to create your first folder?

1. Click the **Quick Create** button in the sidebar (or the **Add Folder** button)
2. Type a name for your folder in the dialog
3. Optionally add a description
4. Click **Create**

That's it! Your folder appears in the list.

## Two Ways to View

CanonCore offers two views for your folders:

- **Tree View** - See your folders as a nested hierarchy (like a file explorer)
- **Grid View** - See your folders as visual cards

Switch between them using the toggle in the top-right corner of your folder list.

## Organizing with Drag and Drop

Want to rearrange your folders? Just drag them:

- Drag up or down to reorder
- Drag onto another folder to nest it inside
- Use the breadcrumbs to navigate back up
