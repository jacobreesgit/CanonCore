Let's take a quick tour of CanonCore so you know where everything is.

## My Items

When you sign in, you land on My Items. This is where all your items live.

### The Sidebar

On the left side, you'll find:

- **My Items** - Takes you back to your main item list
- **Google Drive** - Connect and sync your Drive (via Settings)
- **Get Help** - Opens this documentation
- **Quick Create** - Create a new item from anywhere
- **Your profile** - Sign out and manage your account

### The Header

At the top of the page:

- **Breadcrumbs** - Shows where you are in your item structure
- **Theme toggle** - Switch between light and dark mode

## Your First Item

Ready to create your first item?

1. Click the **Quick Create** button in the sidebar (or the **Add Item** button)
2. Type a name for your item in the dialog
3. Optionally add a description
4. Click **Create**

That's it! Your item appears in the list.

## Two Ways to View

CanonCore offers two views for your items:

- **Tree View** - See your items as a nested hierarchy (like a file explorer)
- **Grid View** - See your items as visual cards

Switch between them using the toggle in the top-right corner of your item list.

## Organizing with Drag and Drop

Want to rearrange your items? Just drag them:

- Drag up or down to reorder
- Drag onto another item to nest it inside
- Use the breadcrumbs to navigate back up
