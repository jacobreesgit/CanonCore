Already have an account? Here's how to sign in.

## Sign in to CanonCore

1. Go to the [sign-in page](/sign-in)
2. Enter the email address you used when signing up
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Sign in**

## Stay signed in

CanonCore remembers your session, so you won't need to sign in every time you visit. Your session stays active until you sign out.

## Forgot your password?

No worries - click the **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page, or go directly to the [password reset page](/docs/account/password-reset).

## Can't sign in?

- **Wrong password?** Double-check for typos, especially capital letters
- **Wrong email?** Make sure you're using the same email you signed up with
- **Account not found?** You may need to [create an account](/docs/getting-started/create-account) first
