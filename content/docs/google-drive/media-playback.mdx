## Streaming media

CanonCore streams video and audio files directly from your Google Drive. After syncing, media files are ready to play without downloading first.

## Supported formats

**Video:** MP4, MKV, AVI, M4V, WebM, MOV

**Audio:** MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, OGG

**Subtitles:** SRT, VTT, SUB, ASS

## Play a file

1. Navigate to an item with media files
2. Click on the item to open its detail view
3. Click **Play** on the hero banner

You can also right-click any item in a grid or tree view and select **Play Next** or **Add to Queue** to queue it up without interrupting what's already playing.

## The mini-player

When you start playing, a mini-player bar appears at the bottom of the screen. It stays there as you browse — navigating between pages won't interrupt playback.

The mini-player shows:

- Track artwork, title, and artist
- **Play/pause** and **skip forward/back** buttons
- A **seek bar** with elapsed and remaining time
- **Volume slider** with mute toggle
- **Shuffle** and **repeat** toggles
- A **queue** button to open the queue panel
- An **expand** button to open the full viewport

## Expanded view

Click the expand button on the mini-player to open the full viewport:

- **Video** fills the screen with the Vidstack player and full controls
- **Audio with artwork** shows the album art with a blurred backdrop
- **Audio without artwork** shows an animated gradient background

The mini-player controls remain available at the bottom of the expanded view. Click **collapse** or press **Escape** to return to the mini-player bar.

To go full-screen, click the fullscreen button or press **F** whilst in the expanded view.

## Queue management

Build a queue on the fly without creating a playlist:

1. Right-click an item and select **Play Next** to insert it after the current track
2. Right-click an item and select **Add to Queue** to append it to the end
3. Click the **queue** button on the mini-player to open the queue panel

In the queue panel you can:

- See the **now playing** track and the **up next** list
- **Drag to reorder** tracks in the queue
- **Remove** individual tracks
- **Clear** the entire queue (with an undo toast so you can restore it)

## Shuffle and repeat

Toggle shuffle and repeat from the mini-player controls:

- **Shuffle** picks the next track randomly when you skip forward or back
- **Repeat** cycles through three modes: off → repeat all → repeat one

## Keyboard shortcuts

These shortcuts work anywhere in the app, as long as you're not typing in a text field:

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Play / pause |
| **M** | Mute / unmute |
| **Left arrow** | Seek back 10 seconds |
| **Right arrow** | Seek forward 10 seconds |
| **Up arrow** | Volume up |
| **Down arrow** | Volume down |
| **F** | Toggle fullscreen (expanded view only) |

## OS media controls

CanonCore integrates with your operating system's media controls. When a track is playing, you can control playback from:

- Your lock screen
- The notification centre
- The macOS Touch Bar or media keys on your keyboard

Track metadata (title, artist, artwork) appears automatically.

## Resume playback

CanonCore saves your playback position automatically every 30 seconds and whenever you switch tabs. When you return to a file, it picks up from where you left off.

## Subtitles

If your item contains subtitle files, they appear in the player's subtitle menu. Click the subtitle icon in the player controls to choose a track.

For best results, keep subtitle files in the same Google Drive folder as the video.

## Primary files

When an item has multiple media files, you can choose which one plays by default:

1. Right-click the item and select **Edit Item**
2. Find the **Primary Media** dropdown
3. Select the file you want as primary

The primary file plays when you click **Play** from the item detail view.

## Image artwork

Items can display artwork on their detail page hero banner:

1. Open **Edit Item** for the item
2. Find the **Hero Artwork** dropdown
3. Select an image file to display

The hero image appears as a cinematic banner when you open the item.

## Troubleshooting

**Video won't play?** Some codecs inside container formats (like certain MKV files) may not work in all browsers. Try a different browser or re-encode the video to H.264.

**Subtitles not showing?** Make sure the subtitle file has a supported extension (.srt, .vtt, .sub, .ass) and is synced with the item.

**Video buffering slowly?** Check your internet connection. Google Drive streaming performance depends on your connection speed and the file size.

**Mini-player not appearing?** The mini-player only shows when a track is actively loaded. Click **Play** on an item to start playback.
