## How syncing works

CanonCore syncs with the "CanonCore" folder in your Google Drive. When you create items or upload files in CanonCore, they appear in Drive. When you add files to Drive, they sync to CanonCore.

## Automatic sync

Items sync automatically when you:

- Create a new item in CanonCore
- Upload files to an item
- Rename or move items
- Delete items

You don't need to manually sync - changes flow to Google Drive in the background.

## Sync status indicators

Items show their sync status with visual indicators:

- **No badge** - Synced with Google Drive (clean UI)
- **Animated spinner** - Sync in progress
- **Small dot** - Waiting to sync
- **Warning triangle** - Sync error (hover to see details)

## What gets synced

**From CanonCore to Drive:**

- New items become folders in Drive
- Uploaded files appear in the matching folder
- Renames update the folder name in Drive
- Deletions move items to Drive's trash

**From Drive to CanonCore:**

- Folders become items with their hierarchy preserved
- Video files (MP4, MKV, etc.) become playable media
- Images become artwork thumbnails
- Subtitles (SRT, VTT) link to their videos

## Manual sync

Click the **Sync** button in the toolbar to trigger an immediate sync. This syncs your entire CanonCore folder from Google Drive, regardless of which page you're on. Whether you're at the root or deep in a subfolder, the sync always pulls all changes from your connected Drive.

If you add content directly to Google Drive, use the Sync button to see it immediately, or wait for the next automatic sync.

## Working offline

CanonCore keeps working even without an internet connection. When you're offline:

- **Operations queue locally** - Creating, renaming, or deleting items queues in your browser
- **Auto-retry on reconnect** - When you come back online, queued operations retry automatically

The queue holds up to 100 pending operations. Each operation retries up to 5 times with increasing delays between attempts. If something fails repeatedly, it appears in your sync history with an error.

## Sync history

View your recent sync activity in **Settings > Activity**. The activity panel shows:

- What synced (folder created, file uploaded, item renamed)
- When it happened
- Whether it succeeded or failed
- Error messages for failed operations

This helps you troubleshoot sync issues and confirm that changes went through.

## File organisation

CanonCore mirrors your Drive folder structure:

- Top-level folders in "CanonCore" become root items
- Subfolders become child items
- Files attach to their parent folder's item

Keep your media organised in Drive, and that organisation carries over to CanonCore.

## Troubleshooting

**Items not appearing?** Make sure files are in the "CanonCore" folder in your Google Drive. Content outside this folder won't sync.

**"Folder is in Trash" warning?** If you accidentally moved your CanonCore folder to Google Drive's Trash, sync pauses until you restore it. Open Google Drive, go to Trash, find the CanonCore folder, and restore it. Then click **Sync** again.

**Sync stuck?** Check your internet connection. If the issue persists, try disconnecting and reconnecting your Google Drive in Settings.

**Error showing?** Hover over the warning triangle to see what went wrong. Common issues include permission changes or network timeouts.
