---
title: Track Your Progress
description: See what you've watched and pick up where you left off
---

CanonCore tracks your viewing progress across your entire library. Progress bars show at a glance how much you've watched in any folder or collection.

## How progress works

Progress is calculated based on your watch history and primary media files:

- An item counts as "watched" when you've seen 80% or more of its primary media file — matching the Trakt industry standard
- When playback crosses the 80% mark, CanonCore automatically records it as watched (called auto-scrobble)
- Folders show combined progress across all items inside them, including nested folders
- You can also manually mark items as watched or unwatched at any time

## Watch status

Every item has a watch status based on your watch history. You can manage this from several places:

**From the context menu** — Right-click (or long-press on mobile) any item and choose **Mark as Watched** or **Mark as Unwatched**.

**From the item detail page** — The hero banner shows a watched/unwatched toggle button.

**Batch operations** — Right-click a folder and choose **Mark All as Watched** to mark every item inside it (including nested folders) as watched in one go. **Mark All as Unwatched** does the reverse.

When you mark an item as unwatched, only the most recent watch record is removed — your older play history is preserved.

## Auto-scrobble

When you're watching a video and playback reaches 80%, CanonCore automatically records it as watched. You don't need to do anything — it happens in the background.

A 5-minute deduplication window prevents duplicate entries if you seek back and replay a section, so you won't get multiple watch records from the same viewing session.

## Where progress appears

You'll see progress indicators in several places:

**Item cards (grid view)** — A thin progress bar at the bottom of each card shows completion percentage. Below the title, you'll see counts like "5/10 watched (of 15 items)".

**Item list (tree view)** — A small progress bar appears next to each item name.

**Item detail pages** — The hero banner shows a progress bar with your watch count.

## Reading the progress display

The progress label tells you three things:

| Display         | Meaning                                                               |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `5/10 watched`  | 5 items with media have been watched out of 10 total items with media |
| `(of 15 items)` | There are 15 total items in this folder (including empty ones)        |
| Progress bar    | Visual percentage of watched vs total                                 |

## What counts as a media file

Only primary media files affect progress:

- **Counted**: The main video or audio file you've marked as primary
- **Not counted**: Trailers, extras, artwork, subtitles

If an item has multiple media files, set the main one as primary in **Settings** so progress tracks correctly.

## Progress and folders

Folders aggregate progress from everything inside:

- A "TV Shows" folder shows progress across all shows and episodes
- A show folder shows progress across all its seasons and episodes
- Progress rolls up through your entire folder hierarchy

## Continue watching

The hero banner shows a **Next Up** button when you have unwatched content. Click it to jump directly to the next item you haven't finished.

**How it works:**

- The button shows "Next Up: [Item Name]" with the name of your next unwatched item
- CanonCore finds the first incomplete item by going through folders in order, checking children before siblings
- An item is "incomplete" if it doesn't have a watch record

**Where the Next Up button appears:**

| Scenario                           | What you see                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Folder with unwatched content      | Next Up: [Item Name] button  |
| Item with media and unwatched kids | Both Play/Resume and Next Up |
| All content watched                | No Next Up button            |
| Profile page with unwatched items  | Next Up: [Item Name] in hero |

This lets you quickly continue watching without manually finding where you left off.

## Edit mode

Progress bars are hidden when you enter edit mode for drag-and-drop reordering. They reappear when you click **Done**.
