title: Track Your Progress
description: See what you've watched and pick up where you left off
---

CanonCore tracks your viewing progress across your entire library. Progress bars show at a glance how much you've watched in any folder or collection.

## How progress works

Progress is calculated based on your primary media files:

- An item counts as "watched" when you've seen 90% or more of its primary media file
- Folders show combined progress across all items inside them, including nested folders
- Progress updates automatically as you watch

## Where progress appears

You'll see progress indicators in several places:

**Item cards (grid view)** - A thin progress bar at the bottom of each card shows completion percentage. Below the title, you'll see counts like "5/10 watched (of 15 items)".

**Item list (tree view)** - A small progress bar appears next to each item name.

**Item detail pages** - The hero banner shows a progress bar with your watch count.

## Reading the progress display

The progress label tells you three things:

| Display         | Meaning                                                               |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `5/10 watched`  | 5 items with media have been watched out of 10 total items with media |
| `(of 15 items)` | There are 15 total items in this folder (including empty ones)        |
| Progress bar    | Visual percentage of watched vs total                                 |

## What counts as a media file

Only primary media files affect progress:

- **Counted**: The main video or audio file you've marked as primary
- **Not counted**: Trailers, extras, artwork, subtitles

If an item has multiple media files, set the main one as primary in **Settings** so progress tracks correctly.

## Progress and folders

Folders aggregate progress from everything inside:

- A "TV Shows" folder shows progress across all shows and episodes
- A show folder shows progress across all its seasons and episodes
- Progress rolls up through your entire folder hierarchy

## Edit mode

Progress bars are hidden when you enter edit mode for drag-and-drop reordering. They reappear when you click **Done**.
