---
title: Create and Manage Playlists
description: Build themed collections that reference items from anywhere in your library
---

Playlists let you group items from anywhere in your library into themed collections — "Weekend Watchlist", "Studio Ghibli Marathon", or whatever you like. Items stay in their original location; playlists just reference them.

## Create a playlist

1. Open any item's detail page
2. Click **Add to Playlist** in the hero section
3. In the dialogue, click **Create new playlist** (or **Create your first playlist** if you don't have any yet)
4. Enter a name and optional description
5. Choose a visibility level (see below)
6. Optionally select items from your library to include
7. Click **Create**

### Choose visibility

The creation dialogue shows three visibility options:

- **Private** (default) — only you can see the playlist
- **Unlisted** — accessible via a share link, but not listed on Explore
- **Public** — visible on your profile and the Explore page

You can change visibility later when editing the playlist. For more details, see [Share Playlists](/docs/playlists/share-playlist).

### Add items during creation

Below the visibility picker, you'll see a searchable list of your library items. Click items to select them — they'll be added to the playlist when you click **Create**.

This is optional. You can always [add items later](/docs/playlists/add-items) from any item's detail page.

## Edit a playlist

1. Open the playlist detail page
2. Click **Edit** in the hero section
3. You can change:
   - **Artwork** — upload a custom cover image or remove the existing one
   - **Name** and **Description**
   - **Public** toggle — make the playlist visible on your profile and the Explore page
   - **Shareable link** — enable or disable link sharing, copy the link, or regenerate it
4. Save your changes

You can also right-click a playlist card to access quick actions like **Rename**, **Make Public** / **Make Private**, and **Delete**.

## Custom artwork

Each playlist can have a custom cover image. If you don't upload one, the playlist card shows a mosaic of up to four item artworks from the playlist.

To upload artwork, open the **Edit** dialogue on the playlist detail page and use the upload button in the artwork area. You can also drag an image onto the drop zone.

To remove artwork, click the remove button on the artwork preview. The card reverts to the automatic mosaic.

## Delete a playlist

You can delete a playlist from two places:

- **Playlist detail page** — click **Delete** in the hero section and confirm
- **Context menu** — right-click a playlist card, select **Delete**, and confirm

Deleting a playlist doesn't affect the items inside it — they stay in their original locations in your library.

## Find your playlists

Your playlists appear in several places:

- **Profile page** — select the **Playlists** tab to see all your playlists
- **Spotlight Search** — press `/` and search for playlist names
- **Explore page** — public playlists appear under the **Playlists** tab
