title: Sign Out
description: Log out of your CanonCore account
---

When you're done using CanonCore, you can sign out to keep your account secure.

## How to sign out

**On desktop:**

1. Look at the bottom-left corner of the sidebar
2. Click on your profile (shows your email)
3. Click **Log out** from the menu

**On mobile:**

1. Tap **Account** in the bottom navigation bar
2. Tap **Sign Out** in the menu that appears

## When to sign out

Consider signing out when:

- You're using a shared or public computer
- You're done for the day and want extra security
- You want to switch to a different account

## What happens when you sign out

- Your session ends immediately
- You'll be taken back to the home page
- Your folders and data remain safe - nothing is deleted
- You'll need to sign in again next time you visit

## Signed out by accident?

Just [sign back in](/sign-in) with your email and password. Everything will be right where you left it.
