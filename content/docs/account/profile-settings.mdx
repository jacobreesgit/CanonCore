---
title: Profile Settings
description: Customise your profile, change your password, and update your email
---

Make CanonCore feel like yours. Change your display name, add images, update your password, or change your email address all from profile settings.

## Open profile settings

**On desktop:**

1. Click your name in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu

**On mobile:**

1. Tap **Account** in the bottom navigation bar
2. Your settings open directly as a bottom sheet

Swipe between the **Profile**, **Account**, **Cloud**, **Prefs**, and **Activity** tabs to find what you need. Password and email changes are inside the **Account** tab.

## Change your display name

1. Click in the **Name** field
2. Type your preferred display name
3. Click **Save Changes**

Your name appears in the sidebar and anywhere your profile is shown.

## Upload an avatar

Your avatar appears next to your name in the sidebar.

1. Drag an image onto the profile picture area, or click the area to browse
2. Click **Save Changes**

**Image requirements:**

- Maximum 1MB file size
- Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP

## Upload a hero banner

Your hero image displays as a full-width banner on your profile page.

1. Drag an image onto the hero banner area, or click the area to browse
2. Click **Save Changes**

**Image requirements:**

- Maximum 2MB file size
- Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP
- Wider images work best for the banner layout

## Remove images

To remove your avatar or hero banner:

1. Open profile settings
2. Click **Remove** next to the image you want to delete
3. Click **Save Changes**

## Change your password

1. Open profile settings
2. Click **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password twice to confirm
5. Click **Change Password**

**Password requirements:**

- At least 8 characters
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include at least one number

## Change your email address

1. Open profile settings
2. Click **Change Email**
3. Enter your new email address
4. Enter your current password to verify
5. Click **Change Email**

Your password is required to confirm email changes for security.

## Export your data or delete your account

The **Account** tab also lets you [download a copy of all your data or permanently delete your account](/docs/account/your-data).
