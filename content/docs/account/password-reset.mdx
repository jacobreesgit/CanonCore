---
title: Reset Your Password
description: Recover access to your account if you forgot your password
---

Forgot your password? No problem - you can reset it in a few steps.

## Request a password reset

1. Go to the [forgot password page](/forgot-password)
2. Enter the email address you used to sign up
3. Click **Send reset link**
4. Check your email inbox for a message from CanonCore

## Reset your password

1. Open the email and click the reset link
2. Enter your new password (must meet the same requirements as before)
3. Confirm your new password
4. Click **Reset password**

## Important notes

- Reset links expire after **30 minutes** for security
- If you don't see the email, check your spam folder
- You can only use each reset link once
- After resetting, you'll need to sign in with your new password

## Didn't receive the email?

- Wait a few minutes - emails can sometimes be delayed
- Check your spam or junk folder
- Make sure you entered the correct email address
- Try requesting a new reset link

## Still having trouble?

If you're unable to reset your password, make sure you're using the same email address you originally signed up with.
