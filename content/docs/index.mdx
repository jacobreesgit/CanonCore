## What is CanonCore?

CanonCore is an organization tool that helps you keep your digital life tidy. Create items, organize them into hierarchies, and find what you need quickly.

## Key Features

- **Item Organization** - Create items and nest them inside each other to build your perfect structure
- **SFTP Connections** - Connect to remote servers and sync items bidirectionally
- **Two View Modes** - Switch between tree view for hierarchy or grid view for visual browsing
- **Drag and Drop** - Rearrange your items by simply dragging them where you want
- **Dark Mode** - Easy on the eyes with automatic light and dark themes
