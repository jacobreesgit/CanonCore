## What is CanonCore?

CanonCore is an organisation tool that helps you keep your digital life tidy. Create items, organise them into hierarchies, and find what you need quickly.

## Key Features

- **Item Organisation** - Create items and nest them inside each other to build your perfect structure
- **Movie & TV Metadata** - Search for films and shows to automatically add posters, descriptions, cast, and more from TMDB
- **Google Drive Integration** - Connect your Drive to sync items and stream media
- **Two View Modes** - Switch between tree view for hierarchy or grid view for visual browsing
- **Drag and Drop** - Rearrange your items by simply dragging them where you want
- **Cinematic Design** - Dark interface optimised for viewing media artwork
