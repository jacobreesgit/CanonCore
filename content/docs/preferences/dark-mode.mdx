---
title: Dark Mode
description: Switch between light and dark themes
---

CanonCore supports both light and dark themes. Switch between them anytime based on your preference or environment.

## Toggle dark mode

1. Look for the sun/moon icon in the header bar (top of the page)
2. Click it to switch themes

- **Sun icon visible** - You're in light mode, click to switch to dark
- **Moon icon visible** - You're in dark mode, click to switch to light

## System preference

When you first use CanonCore, it automatically matches your system's theme preference:

- If your computer is set to dark mode, CanonCore starts in dark mode
- If your computer is set to light mode, CanonCore starts in light mode

Once you manually toggle the theme, your choice is remembered.

## Your preference is saved

CanonCore remembers your theme choice:

- It persists across page reloads
- It applies to all pages including documentation
- It stays set until you change it again

## When to use dark mode

**Dark mode is great for:**

- Working in low-light environments
- Reducing eye strain at night
- Saving battery on OLED screens
- Personal preference

**Light mode is great for:**

- Well-lit environments
- Bright screens
- Matching other apps you use
- Personal preference

## Theme in documentation

The documentation pages (like this one) also respect your theme choice. Switch once, and it applies everywhere.
