---
title: Default Settings
description: Set your preferred view mode and sort order
---

Configure defaults for how your items appear when you sign in. These preferences sync across devices with your account.

## Open preferences

1. Click your avatar or name in the bottom-left corner
2. Click **Settings**
3. Click the **Preferences** tab

## Choose your default view

Pick which view you see when opening your items:

- **Grid** - Visual cards with artwork (default)
- **Tree** - Compact hierarchical list

This sets the starting view. You can still switch between views anytime using the toggle in the toolbar.

## Choose your default sort

Pick how items are ordered when you sign in:

- **Custom Order** - Your manual arrangement (default)
- **Name A-Z** - Alphabetical
- **Name Z-A** - Reverse alphabetical
- **Newest First** - Recently created first
- **Oldest First** - Oldest items first
- **Recently Updated** - Most recent changes first

This sets the starting sort. You can change the sort anytime using the sort dropdown in the toolbar.

## How preferences sync

Your default view and sort preferences are stored with your account. When you sign in on a different device, these defaults apply automatically. Changes you make during a session (like switching views) are saved locally and don't change your account defaults.
