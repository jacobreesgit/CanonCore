---
title: Item Settings
description: Configure item names, descriptions, TMDB metadata, and primary files
---

The Settings dialogue lets you configure items - rename them, add descriptions, and choose which files play or display by default.

## Open Settings

### From the hero area

When you're inside an item (on its detail page):

1. Click the **Settings** button in the hero area (next to the play and playlist buttons)
2. The Settings dialogue opens

On mobile, settings open as a bottom sheet with swipeable tabs for **Details**, **Files** (when Google Drive is connected), and **TMDB** (when the item has TMDB metadata).

### From the context menu

From any view (grid or tree):

1. Right-click on any item
2. Select **Settings** from the menu

This works whether you're on the root page or browsing nested items.

## What you can do

### Rename the item

1. Update the **Name** field
2. Click **Save** to apply

The change takes effect immediately. Renaming doesn't affect the item's contents or nested items.

### Add a description

Short descriptions help you remember what's in an item or add context.

1. Type in the **Description** field (up to 1000 characters)
2. Click **Save** to apply

The character count updates as you type. Leave the field empty to remove an existing description.

Descriptions appear below item names in both grid and tree views when browsing (not while dragging).

### Using the name field

The name field works two ways: as a simple text input for renaming, and as a search box for The Movie Database (TMDB).

**For simple renaming**: Just type the new name and click **Save**. TMDB results may appear as you type, but you can ignore them.

**For movies and TV shows**: Click a TMDB result to open the metadata wizard with poster options and descriptions.

### Look up movie or TV metadata

If you want to use TMDB data:

1. Start typing in the **Name** field
2. Results appear with poster thumbnails and release years
3. Click a result to open the metadata wizard

**For TV shows**, you can drill down to a specific level before continuing:

- **Use Entire Show** - Apply show-level metadata (e.g., "Breaking Bad")
- **Select a Season** - Click a season to see its episodes, or use that season's metadata
- **Select an Episode** - Choose a specific episode for episode-level metadata (e.g., "S1E1: Pilot")

Episodes don't have artwork on TMDB, so selecting an episode skips the poster and hero steps.

**For movies**, clicking a result goes straight to the metadata wizard.

The wizard walks you through up to four steps:

**Step 1: Title & Description**
Choose whether to update the name and/or description. You'll see your current values alongside the TMDB data, so you can decide what to keep.

**Step 2: Poster Selection**
Browse available poster images from TMDB, sorted by community rating. With Google Drive connected, you can also switch to the "My Uploads" tab to use your own artwork. You can skip this step entirely if you don't want a poster.

**Step 3: Hero Selection**
Choose a backdrop image for the hero banner. These are widescreen images that appear at the top of item detail pages. Same options: pick from TMDB, upload your own (with Drive), or skip.

**Step 4: Review & Apply**
See a summary of everything you've selected — text changes and artwork — before applying. Click the poster or hero image to change your selection, or use the **Clear** buttons to remove artwork choices. When you're happy, click **Apply Changes** to save.

TMDB posters and backdrops are served directly from TMDB's servers, so you don't need Google Drive connected for them to display. Google Drive is only needed if you want to upload your own custom artwork.

**Tip**: You can skip image steps if you only want to update the text fields, or use the "Skip All" button to jump straight to the review step.

### Control which metadata sections appear

When you apply TMDB metadata to an item, the detail page can show several sections — cast, genres, streaming providers, and videos. You can toggle each section on or off per item.

The available toggles are:

- **Tagline** - The film or show's tagline (e.g., "Long live the fighters.")
- **Metadata** - Year, runtime, rating, and content rating
- **Genres** - Genre tags like Action, Drama, Sci-Fi
- **Cast** - Cast members with profile photos
- **Where to Watch** - Streaming providers (Netflix, Disney+, etc.)
- **Videos** - Trailers and behind-the-scenes clips

All sections are enabled by default. Turn off any section you don't want to see on that item's detail page. These preferences are saved per item, so you can show full metadata for a film but hide it for a folder.

There are two places to set these:

**During the metadata wizard** — The review step (step 4) shows the toggles before you apply changes. Good for setting preferences when you first add metadata.

**From the TMDB tab in Settings** — Once an item has TMDB metadata, the Settings dialogue shows a **TMDB** tab alongside Details and Files. Open it to adjust display options at any time. Changes save automatically — no need to click Save.

Display preferences carry over when someone forks your item.

### Change individual artwork

Once an item has TMDB metadata, you can swap out its poster, backdrop, or episode still without re-running the full wizard.

1. Open the **TMDB** tab in Settings
2. Click the artwork field you want to change (Poster, Backdrop, or Still)
3. Browse the available images from TMDB
4. Select an image, then click **Apply**

The gallery shows images sorted by community rating. Movies and TV shows have poster and backdrop fields. Seasons have a poster field. Episodes have a still field.

To remove a single artwork image without detaching all metadata, click the trash icon on that artwork field. The other TMDB data stays intact.

### Remove TMDB metadata

If you want to completely detach an item from TMDB:

1. Open the **Details** tab in Settings
2. Find the TMDB source field (it shows the linked movie or TV show)
3. Click the trash icon
4. Confirm in the dialogue that appears

Detaching removes the TMDB link, poster, backdrop, and resets display options to defaults. The item's name and description stay as they are — you won't lose any text you've written.

After detaching, the TMDB tab disappears from Settings. You can always re-link by searching for a title in the name field again.

### Artwork overrides

When an item has TMDB artwork, you'll see a badge on the Files tab saying "Currently using TMDB poster. Upload to override." Uploading your own artwork through Google Drive takes precedence over the TMDB image. On the TMDB tab, a badge reading "Overridden by uploaded artwork" confirms your upload is being used instead. If you remove the uploaded file, the TMDB artwork becomes active again.

### When TMDB has no results

If TMDB doesn't recognise what you're searching for, you'll see "No results found". This is expected for non-movie/TV content like folders, games, or books. Just type your desired name and click **Save** - the search is optional.

### Choose primary files

When an item has multiple files of the same type, you can pick which one is the "primary" - the one that plays or displays by default.

The Settings dialogue shows sections for:

- **Primary Media** - Which video or audio file plays first
- **Primary Artwork** - Which image shows as the thumbnail
- **Hero Artwork** - Which image appears in the large hero banner
- **Default Subtitle** - Which subtitle track loads automatically

The Primary Media, Artwork, and Subtitle sections appear when you have files of that type. With only one file, the dropdown shows your file but is disabled. With 2+ files, you can select which one to use.

The Hero Artwork section only appears when you have 2 or more artwork files, since it lets you choose a different image for the banner than the thumbnail.

**Primary vs Hero artwork**: Primary artwork is used for thumbnails in grid view. Hero artwork is used for the large banner at the top of item detail pages. You can use the same image for both, or choose different ones.

## File summary

At the top of the dialogue, you'll see icons showing how many files are attached:

- Media files (video, audio)
- Artwork files (images)
- Subtitle files

## Close the dialogue

Click the **X** button or press **Escape** to close. If you haven't saved name changes, they won't be applied.

On mobile, swipe the sheet down to close it. If you have unsaved changes, you'll be asked whether to discard them or keep editing.
