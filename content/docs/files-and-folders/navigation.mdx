title: Navigate Your Items
description: Move through your item structure with ease
---

As your item collection grows, knowing how to navigate becomes essential.

## Using breadcrumbs

The breadcrumb trail at the top of the page shows where you are:

```
My Items > Work > Projects > Project A
```

- Click any breadcrumb to jump directly to that item
- Click **My Items** to return to the top level

## Opening items

To see what's inside an item:

- **In tree view**: Click the item name or the arrow to expand
- **In grid view**: Click on the item card

## Going back

Several ways to navigate back:

- Click a breadcrumb in the header
- Click **My Items** in the sidebar
- Use your browser's back button

## Keyboard shortcuts

For power users:

- **/** - Open spotlight search
- **Enter** - Open the selected item
- **Escape** - Cancel current action or close search
- **Arrow keys** - Navigate between items (in tree view or search results)

## Search for items

Press **/** anywhere in the app to open spotlight search. Start typing to find items instantly.

The search dialog shows:

- **Artwork thumbnails** next to each result (if the item has artwork)
- **Breadcrumb paths** for nested items (e.g., "Movies / Star Wars")
- **Fuzzy matching** so you don't need exact names

Click a result (or press Enter) to jump directly to that item.

You can also click the **Search** button in the sidebar if you prefer using your mouse.

## Pin items for quick access

Frequently used items can be pinned to the sidebar for one-click navigation.

### Pin an item

1. Right-click any item (in tree or grid view)
2. Select **Pin to Sidebar**
3. The item appears in a new "Pinned" section below the main navigation

### Unpin an item

Two ways to unpin:

- Right-click the item (anywhere) and select **Unpin from Sidebar**
- Right-click the pinned item in the sidebar and select **Unpin from Sidebar**

### Pinned items limits

- You can pin up to 10 items
- Pinned items show in the order you pinned them (earliest first)
- Pinning an already-pinned item does nothing (it stays in place)
- Deleting a pinned item removes it from both the sidebar and your items

### What's in the sidebar context menu

When you right-click a pinned item in the sidebar:

- **Settings** - Open the item's settings dialog
- **Unpin from Sidebar** - Remove from pinned items
- **Delete** - Delete the item entirely (with confirmation)

## Lost?

Click **My Items** in the sidebar to always get back to your starting point.
