title: Navigate Your Folders
description: Move through your folder structure with ease
---

As your folder collection grows, knowing how to navigate becomes essential.

## Using breadcrumbs

The breadcrumb trail at the top of the page shows where you are:

```
My Files > Work > Projects > Project A
```

- Click any breadcrumb to jump directly to that folder
- Click **My Files** to return to the top level

## Opening folders

To see what's inside a folder:

- **In tree view**: Click the folder name or the arrow to expand
- **In grid view**: Click on the folder card

## Going back

Several ways to navigate back:

- Click a breadcrumb in the header
- Click **My Files** in the sidebar
- Use your browser's back button

## Keyboard shortcuts

For power users:

- **Enter** - Open the selected folder
- **Escape** - Cancel current action
- **Arrow keys** - Navigate between items (in tree view)

## Finding folders quickly

If you have many folders:

- Use clear, searchable names when creating folders
- Keep your most-used folders near the top
- Consider organizing by category or project

## Lost?

Click **My Files** in the sidebar to always get back to your starting point.
