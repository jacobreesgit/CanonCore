As your item collection grows, knowing how to navigate becomes essential.

## Using breadcrumbs

The breadcrumb trail at the top of the page shows where you are:

```
My Items > Work > Projects > Project A
```

- Click any breadcrumb to jump directly to that item
- Click **My Items** to return to the top level

## Opening items

To see what's inside an item:

- **In tree view**: Click the item name or the arrow to expand
- **In grid view**: Click on the item card

## Inside an item

When you open an item, you'll see a detail page with a hero banner and any child items listed below.

If the item has TMDB metadata or child items, the page splits into two tabs:

- **Contents** — Child items with the same sort, filter, and view controls as the main page
- **About** — TMDB metadata sections like cast, genres, streaming providers, and recommendations

**On mobile**, swipe left or right to switch between the tabs.

You can control which About sections appear per item from [item settings](/docs/files-and-folders/item-settings#control-which-metadata-sections-appear).

## Going back

Several ways to navigate back:

- Click a breadcrumb in the header
- Click **My Items** in the sidebar (on mobile, tap **My Items** in the bottom navigation bar)
- Use your browser's back button

## Keyboard shortcuts

For power users:

- **/** - Open spotlight search
- **Enter** - Open the selected item
- **Escape** - Cancel current action or close search
- **Arrow keys** - Navigate between items (in tree view or search results)

## Search for items

**On desktop:** Press **/** anywhere in the app to open spotlight search. You can also click the **Search** button in the sidebar.

**On mobile:** Tap **Search** in the bottom navigation bar to open the search sheet.

### What you'll find in search

The search dialogue shows four sections:

- **Your Items** - Items from your own library (signed-in users only)
- **Playlists** - Your playlists with artwork and item counts
- **Public Collections** - Public items shared by other users
- **People** - Public profiles you can visit

For each result, you'll see:

- **Poster thumbnails** next to each result (items with TMDB metadata show their poster automatically; items with custom artwork show that instead)
- **Breadcrumb paths** for nested items (e.g., "Movies / Star Wars")
- **Owner attribution** for public items (@username)
- **Fuzzy matching** so you don't need exact names

Click a result (or press Enter) to jump directly to that item or profile.

## Pin items for quick access

Frequently used items can be pinned for quick navigation.

### Pin an item

1. Right-click any item (in tree or grid view), or long-press on mobile
2. Select **Pin to Sidebar**

**On desktop:** The item appears as a collapsible submenu under **My Items** in the sidebar.

**On mobile:** Pinned items appear in a dedicated "Pinned" section at the top of grid view.

### Unpin an item

To unpin an item:

- Right-click (or long-press on mobile) the item anywhere and select **Unpin from Sidebar**
### Pinned items limits

- You can pin up to 10 items
- Pinned items show in the order you pinned them (earliest first)
- Pinning an already-pinned item does nothing (it stays in place)
- Deleting a pinned item removes it from both pinned and your items

## Lost?

Click **My Items** in the sidebar (or tap **My Items** on mobile) to always get back to your starting point.
