Browse curated media libraries from other CanonCore users. Find inspiration for organising your own collection or fork items you like.

## Open the Explore page

**On desktop:** Click **Explore** in the sidebar navigation.

**On mobile:** Tap **Explore** in the bottom navigation bar.

The Explore page is available to everyone, whether signed in or not.

## Featured collections

At the top of the Explore page, a cinematic hero carousel highlights recently updated collections with artwork. Featured items are automatically selected based on activity and visual appeal.

When items have TMDB metadata, the carousel displays rich details — tagline, release year, runtime, genres, and content rating. The carousel auto-advances every few seconds. You can also click the navigation dots below to jump to a specific item.

**Quick actions on each slide**:

- **View Item** - Open the item detail page
- **Fork** (signed-in users) - Copy the item to your library without leaving the page

## Browse collections and playlists

The Explore page has two tabs: **Items** and **Playlists**.

### Items tab

A grid displays all explicitly public items from the community. Items that inherit visibility from their parent don't appear here (see [Item Visibility](/docs/sharing/item-visibility)).

Each card displays:

- Item artwork
- Item name
- Owner's username (shown as @username, or "You" for your own items)

Click any item to view it in detail.

### Playlists tab

A grid displays public playlists from all users. Each card shows the playlist's custom artwork or a mosaic of item posters, along with the playlist name and owner.

Click any playlist to view its contents. To learn more about playlists, see [Create and Manage Playlists](/docs/playlists/create-playlist).

## Search for content

Both the Items and Playlists tabs include a search bar below the toolbar. Type to filter results — the search updates as you type with a short delay, and your query is saved in the URL so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

If no results match your search, an empty state appears with the option to clear the search and start again.

## Infinite scroll

Items and playlists load progressively as you scroll down the page. When you reach the bottom, more items appear automatically. There's no need to click through pages — just keep scrolling.

## Sort and filter items

Use the toolbar to control which items you see and how they're ordered.

### Sort

Use the sort dropdown to change how items appear:

- **Recently Updated** - Most recently modified (default)
- **Name A-Z** - Alphabetical order
- **Name Z-A** - Reverse alphabetical

Your sort preference is saved in the URL.

### Exclude your own items

When signed in, an **Exclude Mine** toggle button appears in the toolbar. Click it to hide your own items from the grid, making it easier to discover new content from other users. The toggle highlights when active.

Your sort preference and exclude setting are saved in the URL, so you can bookmark a filtered view.

## Right-click actions on items

When signed in, you can right-click (or long-press on mobile) any item on the Explore grid for quick actions.

**On your own items**, the context menu includes:

- **Edit Item** - Open the item settings
- **Pin / Unpin** - Toggle sidebar pinning
- **Play Next** - Insert into the playback queue after the current track
- **Add to Queue** - Append to the end of the playback queue
- **Add to Playlist** - Save the item to one of your playlists
- **Delete** - Remove the item

**On other people's items**, the context menu includes:

- **Fork to Library** - Copy the item to your library (see [Fork Items](/docs/sharing/forking))
- **Add to Playlist** - Save the item to one of your playlists

If you've already forked an item, the option shows as "In Your Library" instead.

## View a public profile

To view someone's full public profile:

- On the Explore grid, note the @username on any card
- On an item detail page, click the @username in the breadcrumb navigation

You can also visit any public profile directly at `/u/username`.

## Search with Spotlight

**On desktop:** Press **/** to open spotlight search.

**On mobile:** Tap **Search** in the bottom navigation bar.

Along with your own items (if signed in), you'll see:

- **Public Items** - Public items shared by other users
- **Playlists** - Public playlists from the community
- **People** - Public profiles matching your search

Search is the fastest way to find specific public content or discover new users to follow.

## What you can do

On public profiles and items, you can:

- Browse their public collection hierarchy
- View item details and descriptions
- Fork items to your own library (see [Fork Items](/docs/sharing/forking))
- Add items to your playlists

You cannot:

- Access their files (videos, subtitles)
- See their watch progress
- See their private items
