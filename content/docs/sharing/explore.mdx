Browse curated media libraries from other CanonCore users. Find inspiration for organizing your own collection or fork items you like.

## Open the Explore page

Click **Explore** in the sidebar navigation.

The Explore page is available to everyone, whether signed in or not.

## Browse public collections

The Explore page shows a grid of all public root-level items from the community. Each card displays:

- Item artwork
- Item name
- Owner's username (shown as @username)

Click any item to view it in detail.

## View a public profile

To view someone's full public profile:

- On the Explore grid, note the @username on any card
- On an item detail page, click the @username in the breadcrumb navigation

You can also visit any public profile directly at `/u/username`.

## What you can do

On public profiles and items, you can:

- Browse their public collection hierarchy
- View item details and descriptions
- Fork items to your own library (see [Forking Items](/docs/sharing/forking))

You cannot:

- Access their files (videos, subtitles)
- See their watch progress
- See their private items
