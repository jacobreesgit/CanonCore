---
title: Fork Items
description: Copy public items to your own library
---

Forking lets you copy someone else's public item into your library. Use it to build on curated collections or save items you discover whilst exploring.

## Fork an item

### From the Explore carousel

1. Navigate to the **Explore** page
2. In the featured carousel, find an item you want to fork
3. Click **Fork** on the carousel slide
4. Choose where to place it:
   - **My Items (Root)** - add at the top level
   - Or select any existing folder from the list
5. Click **Fork Here**

### From the Explore or profile grid

1. Right-click (or long-press on mobile) any item you don't own
2. Select **Fork to Library** from the context menu
3. Choose where to place it:
   - **My Items (Root)** - add at the top level
   - Or select any existing folder from the list
4. Click **Fork Here**

If you've already forked an item, the menu shows "In Your Library" instead.

### From an item detail page

1. Navigate to a public item (via Explore or a public profile)
2. Click **Fork to Library**

The item is copied to the top level of your library.

## What gets copied

When you fork an item:

- Name and description
- TMDB metadata (if any)

What doesn't get copied:

- Files (videos, subtitles, artwork)
- Child items
- Watch progress

Forked items start as private.

## Fork attribution

Forked items show where they came from:

- "Forked from [Item Name] by @username"
- The attribution appears on the item page

Public items also display a fork count showing how many times they've been forked.

## Fork restrictions

You cannot fork:

- Your own items
- The same item twice
- Items that aren't fully public (all ancestors must be public)

## After forking

Once an item is in your library, it's completely independent:

- Rename it, add descriptions, reorganise
- Upload your own files
- The original owner's changes don't affect your copy
