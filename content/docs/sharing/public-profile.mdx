---
title: Public Profile
description: Set up a username and make your profile visible to others
---

Share your collections with the community by enabling a public profile. Once public, anyone can view your profile at your unique URL.

## Open settings

1. Click your name in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu

The public profile options are in the Profile tab.

## Choose a username

Your username creates your public profile URL (e.g., canoncore.com/u/yourname).

1. Enter your desired username in the **Username** field
2. Wait for the availability check (shown as you type)
3. Click **Save Changes**

**Username requirements:**

- 3 to 20 characters
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only
- Cannot start or end with an underscore
- Cannot have consecutive underscores
- Case-insensitive (JohnDoe and johndoe are treated as the same)

## Make your profile public

Once you have a username, toggle your profile visibility:

1. Find the **Public Profile** toggle in Settings
2. Switch it on to make your profile discoverable
3. Click **Save Changes**

When your profile is public:

- Your profile appears at `/u/yourname`
- Your public items are visible to anyone
- Others can fork your public items

## Keep your profile private

To hide your profile from the public:

1. Open Settings
2. Switch the **Public Profile** toggle off
3. Click **Save Changes**

Your profile and items will no longer be visible to others, but your username is preserved.
