## What is Google Drive integration?

CanonCore connects to your Google Drive account to sync folders, stream media, and upload files. Once connected, your Drive content appears in CanonCore and changes sync automatically.

## Connect your Google Drive

**On desktop:**

1. Click your profile picture in the sidebar
2. Select **Settings**
3. Find the **Google Drive** section
4. Click **Connect Google Drive**
5. Sign in with your Google account
6. Grant CanonCore access to your Drive

**On mobile:**

1. Tap **Account** in the bottom navigation bar
2. Tap your Google Drive connection status (or **Connect Google Drive** if not connected)
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. Grant CanonCore access to your Drive

After connecting, you'll see a "Connected" badge with your Google email address. A storage bar also appears, showing how much of your Google Drive quota is in use.

## What permissions does CanonCore need?

CanonCore requests access to manage files in Google Drive. This lets it:

- Create a "CanonCore" folder in your Drive
- Read and sync files from that folder
- Upload new files when you add media
- Rename and organise items

CanonCore only accesses files in its own folder - it won't touch your other Drive content.

## The CanonCore folder

When you connect, CanonCore creates a folder called "CanonCore" in your Google Drive. This is where all your synced content lives.

If you already have a CanonCore folder (from a previous connection or created manually), CanonCore reuses it and automatically imports any existing content. You'll see a brief "Syncing existing content..." message while your files are imported.

You can:

- Add files directly in Google Drive and sync them to CanonCore
- Create subfolders in Google Drive to organise your media
- Access the same content from any device with your Google account

## Disconnect Google Drive

To disconnect:

1. Open **Settings** from your profile menu
2. Click the trash icon next to your connected account
3. Confirm the disconnection

**Important:** Disconnecting permanently deletes the CanonCore folder and all its contents from your Google Drive. This can't be undone. If you want to keep your files, move them out of the CanonCore folder in Google Drive before disconnecting.

Your CanonCore access token is also revoked at Google's end, so CanonCore no longer has any access to your Drive after disconnecting.

## Reconnect if needed

If your Google Drive connection expires or has issues, CanonCore shows an amber warning banner across all pages so you don't miss it.

**On desktop**, the banner appears below the breadcrumb bar in the header. **On mobile**, it appears at the top of the screen.

The banner includes a **Reconnect** button. Tap it to reauthorise CanonCore with your Google account — you'll be redirected to Google's sign-in page, and once you grant access again, the banner disappears and syncing resumes.

While the connection is expired, syncing and media streaming are paused. Your files remain safe in Google Drive throughout.

## Storage usage

The storage bar in Settings shows your Google Drive quota:

- **Blue bar** - Normal usage (under 80%)
- **Yellow bar with warning** - Getting full (80-94%)
- **Red bar with warning** - Almost full (95%+)

This helps you know when to clean up old files or upgrade your Drive storage. The storage bar updates after each sync.

When your storage reaches 95% or more, CanonCore blocks new file uploads before they start. You'll see an error message asking you to free up space or upgrade your Google Drive plan before uploading.
