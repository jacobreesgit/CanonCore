---
title: Remembering Your Settings
description: How CanonCore saves your view, sort, and filter choices
---

CanonCore automatically remembers your view mode, sort order, and filter selections — no manual configuration needed.

## How it works

Your choices for sort, filter, and view mode are saved in two places:

1. **URL parameters** — Your current settings appear in the page URL (e.g. `?sort=name-asc&view=grid`). This means you can bookmark or share a specific view.
2. **Browser storage** — When you navigate directly to a page without URL parameters, your last-used settings are restored from local storage.

## What gets saved

- **Sort order** — Which sort option you last selected (e.g. Name A-Z, Newest First)
- **Filters** — Which content filters are active (e.g. Has Files, Synced)
- **View mode** — Grid or Tree view

## Share a specific view

Because settings live in the URL, you can copy the address bar to share a filtered, sorted view with someone else. For example:

```
/u/yourname?sort=name-asc&filter=has-files&view=grid
```

This opens your library sorted alphabetically, filtered to items with files, in grid view.

## Switching devices

Settings are stored in your browser, so they don't sync across devices. When you visit on a new device, you'll start with the defaults (Custom Order, no filters, Grid view) and your choices will be saved from there.
