Getting started with CanonCore takes just a few steps.

## Sign up for an account

1. Go to the [sign-up page](/sign-up)
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a password that meets these requirements:
   - At least 8 characters long
   - Contains at least one uppercase letter
   - Contains at least one lowercase letter
   - Contains at least one number
4. Confirm your password by typing it again
5. Click **Create account**

## Verify your email

After signing up, CanonCore sends a verification email to the address you provided. Click the link in the email to verify your address.

If you don't see it, check your spam folder. You can also resend the verification email from the banner that appears at the top of the page.

## What happens next

After you create your account, you'll be taken straight to My Items. This is your home base where you'll organise all your items.

## Already have an account?

If you've signed up before, head to the [sign-in page](/sign-in) instead.

## Having trouble?

- **Password not accepted?** Make sure it meets all the requirements listed above
- **Email already in use?** You may already have an account - try [signing in](/sign-in) or [resetting your password](/docs/account/password-reset)
