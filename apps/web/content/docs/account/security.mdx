Your account security matters. Here's how CanonCore protects your data and how you can help.

## How CanonCore protects you

### Password security

- Your password is encrypted and never stored in plain text
- CanonCore uses industry-standard hashing (bcrypt) to protect it
- Even CanonCore can't see your actual password

### Account lockout

If someone tries to guess your password, CanonCore locks the account after 5 failed attempts. The lockout lasts 15 minutes, and the sign-in page shows how long you need to wait.

This protects against automated password guessing without permanently locking you out.

### Email verification

When you create an account, CanonCore sends a verification email. Click the link to confirm your address.

If you change your email in [profile settings](/docs/account/profile-settings), CanonCore sends a verification link to the **new** address. Your email won't change until you verify it — this prevents someone from hijacking your account by changing your email without access to the new inbox.

If you haven't verified your email yet, you'll see a banner at the top of the page with a **Resend** button.

### Session protection

- Your login session is secured with encrypted tokens
- Sessions are tied to your browser for extra safety
- Changing or resetting your password automatically signs out all other sessions, so if someone has access to your account, changing your password locks them out immediately

### Rate limiting

- Too many failed login attempts? CanonCore slows down requests to prevent attacks
- This protects against automated password guessing

## How you can help

### Choose a strong password

- Use at least 8 characters
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Add at least one number
- Avoid common words or personal information

### Keep your password private

- Don't share your password with anyone
- Don't use the same password for other accounts
- Consider using a password manager

### Sign out on shared devices

- Always sign out when using public computers
- Don't save your password on shared devices

## Suspicious activity?

If you notice anything unusual with your account:

1. [Reset your password](/docs/account/password-reset) immediately — this signs out all other sessions
2. Make sure to use a completely new password
3. Check that your email address is still correct

## Privacy

By default, your folders and data are private to your account. Other people can't see or access your content.

If you choose to [make your profile public](/docs/sharing/public-profile), others can view items you've marked as public. Your private items and actual file contents (videos, subtitles) always remain accessible only to you.
