title: Your Data
description: Download your data or permanently delete your account
---

Your data belongs to you. CanonCore lets you export everything or delete your account entirely — both from the **Account** tab in settings.

## Download your data

Get a complete copy of your account as a JSON file. The export includes your profile, all items with file metadata and TMDB fields, playlists with their item memberships, and fork records.

1. Open **Settings** and switch to the **Account** tab
2. Under **Your Data**, click **Download My Data**
3. A JSON file named `canoncore-export-YYYY-MM-DD.json` downloads to your device

The export doesn't include binary files like artwork images or uploaded media — just the structured data and metadata.

## Delete your account

Account deletion is permanent. Once confirmed, CanonCore removes your account and everything associated with it: items, playlists, files, forks, and sync logs. There's no way to undo this.

1. Open **Settings** and switch to the **Account** tab
2. Under **Danger Zone**, click **Delete Account**
3. Enter your current password
4. Type `DELETE` in the confirmation field
5. Click **Delete My Account**

After deletion, you're signed out and redirected to the homepage.

### What gets deleted

- Your profile (name, email, avatar, hero banner)
- All items and their file records
- All playlists and playlist memberships
- All fork records
- Google Drive connection details
- Sync logs and password reset tokens

### Google Drive files

If you have Google Drive connected, CanonCore moves your CanonCore folder to Google Drive's trash before deleting your account. Your actual media files stay in your Drive — you can restore them from the trash if needed. If the folder can't be trashed (for example, if your Google token has expired), the account deletion still proceeds.

### Before you delete

Consider [downloading your data](#download-your-data) first, so you have a record of your library structure and metadata.
