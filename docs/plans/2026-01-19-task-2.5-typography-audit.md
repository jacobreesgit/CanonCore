**Date:** 2026-01-19
**Status:** Complete
**Rules Applied:** 6 typography rules from web-design-guidelines

---

## Rules Audited

1. **Use ellipsis `…` not `...`** - Use proper Unicode ellipsis character
2. **Use curly quotes, not straight** - Use proper typographic quotes
3. **Non-breaking spaces in measurements/brands** - Use `&nbsp;` or `\u00A0`
4. **Loading states end with `…`** - Loading indicators use ellipsis
5. **Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`** - Numbers align in columns
6. **Use `text-wrap: balance` on headings** - Multi-line headings wrap evenly
